# Checklist before submitting

- [ ] The software or dataset, and the exact version tested, are identified in the header and in *What was examined*.
- [ ] *Why it matters* gives evidence of use or explains the consequences of an error.
- [ ] The test materials (code, data, instructions) are archived publicly, and their DOI is in the `materials` field.
- [ ] Someone other than the authors could rerun the test by following *Reproducing this examination*.
- [ ] *The independent reference* explains why the reference is independent, and declares any shared code, methods or ancestry.
- [ ] *Results* states whether the software or dataset signalled the problem.
- [ ] The maintainers were notified before submission, ideally at least 30 days earlier, and *Maintainers' response* gives a dated account.
- [ ] Any possible security vulnerability has been handled under the project's security policy.
- [ ] Prior reports of the same problem are cited.
- [ ] *Conflicts of interest* and *Use of AI tools* are completed.
- [ ] References are complete, with DOIs where available.
